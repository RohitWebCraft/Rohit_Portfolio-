      How the games help learners

      Every feature comes from real classroom use.

      • English first, Hindi as supportQuestions appear in English, with a short Hindi translation beneath for support.
      • Instant spoken feedbackCorrect and wrong answers get different spoken phrases and sounds, so practice feels like a game.
      • Streaks, XP and ranksDaily streaks, experience points and ranks give learners a reason to come back.
      • Mistakes NotebookMissed questions are saved so learners can revisit them until they get them right.
      • Verifiable certificatesA certificate is awarded after every 10 levels, and a QR code confirms that it is genuine.
      • Teacher dashboardEach learner's progress syncs to a Google Sheet: levels, stars, XP, rank and last active date.
